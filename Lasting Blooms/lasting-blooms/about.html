About Us

Lasting Blooms is a small flower business run by Malaki. We make bouquets and gift boxes for all kinds of occasions.


Our goal is simple: flowers that look beautiful and stay fresh for as long as possible.

What We Offer

Birthdays

Bright bouquets to make someone's day.

Weddings

Flowers for the bride, the tables and the venue.

Gifts

Small boxes and bouquets for any occasion.

How to Order

  1. Pick your flowers from the shop page.
  2. Add them to your cart.
  3. Send us a message with your order and address.
  4. We prepare your flowers and deliver them.
